Philosophy and Approach

Philosophy

A book is not a product to push. It is a story or an idea that deserves the right readers.

Approach

Every project starts with an honest look at where the book stands today: its page, its positioning, its audience.

Why discovery comes first

Promotion sends people to a book. Discovery makes sure that when they arrive, the book is ready to convert them.
